About Me

I'm an Embedded Hardware Engineer with 4 years of professional experience designing cutting-edge PCBs and power circuits for industrial IoT and automation products. My passion lies in transforming complex requirements into reliable, scalable hardware solutions that make a real-world impact.

My expertise spans multi-layer PCB design, schematic capture, power electronics, RF layout, signal integrity analysis, and complete hardware bring-up. I work seamlessly across MCU families including STM32, ESP32, Nuvoton, and Quectel platforms, implementing communication protocols like RS485, CAN, Ethernet, 4G LTE, LoRa, and Zigbee.

I believe in delivering not just functional hardware, but solutions optimized for manufacturing, reliability, and performance. My background in firmware development in C/C++ allows me to bridge the hardware-software boundary effectively, ensuring smooth integration and debugging across the entire system.
