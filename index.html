01 — About

A hardware engineer who ships.

I lead end-to-end PCB design — from schematic capture and component selection through multi-layer layout, power-rail design, signal-integrity checks, prototype bring-up, and validation. My focus is industrial IoT and automation products that have to work reliably in the field, not just on the bench.

I work fluently across MCU families (STM32, ESP32-S3, Nuvoton M03, Quectel EC200U) and the protocols that connect them — RS485, CAN, Ethernet, 4G LTE, LoRa, Zigbee, BLE. A solid firmware background in Embedded C/C++ lets me debug across the hardware-software boundary.
